Our Story

A family farm with a passion for roses

Grace Rose Farm began as a small family plot with a simple dream — to grow the most beautiful garden roses in the world. Today, we cultivate over 40 varieties of luxury roses, each chosen for its fragrance, form, and timeless beauty.

Our roses are grown using sustainable, chemical-free methods in the rich English soil. We believe that the most beautiful flowers come from the most careful hands — and that every stem tells a story of patience, craft, and love.

From weddings and events to floral designers and private clients, our roses bring a touch of English garden romance to life's most beautiful moments.

— The Grace Family

Visit The Farm

Experience the rose fields

Join us at Grace Rose Farm for a truly special experience. Wander through our rose fields in bloom, learn about sustainable rose growing, and discover the varieties that make our roses so beloved by floral designers around the world.

We welcome visitors by appointment during our rose season — June through September. Private tours, workshops, and farm visits are available for small groups.
