Our Atelier

The art of modern floristry

MAISON FLORA was founded on a simple belief — that flowers should feel as considered as the spaces they inhabit. We work with seasonal blooms, natural textures, and a restrained palette to create arrangements that feel both timeless and entirely contemporary.

Each bouquet is arranged by hand in our studio, using flowers sourced from trusted growers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. We believe in slow floristry — taking the time to craft each arrangement with intention and care.

— The Maison Flora Team
